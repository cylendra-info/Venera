## 1. Categorization Matrix

To ensure absolute institutional integrity and prevent unsubstantiated claims from being presented as official fact to government bodies, financial institutions, and partners, all content on the VENERA Technology corporate website is classified into three strict verification tiers:

- **Tier 1: Confirmed Information** — Corroborated by corporate records, public code repositories, published technical papers, or verified external records.
- **Tier 2: Founder-Provided Information** — Formally submitted by Founder Ali Hussein, reflecting professional background, historical experience, and technical vision. Marked as founder attribution or historical context.
- **Tier 3: Unverified / Pending Substantiation** — Requires documentary proof, legal clearance, or metric audits before public marketing claims can be made. Never displayed as verified statistics on the website.
