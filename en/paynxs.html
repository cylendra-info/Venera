PayNXS

Merchant Digital Payments Ecosystem for Emerging Markets

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

PayNXS was an ambitious fintech platform engineered to overcome the cash-heavy retail constraints of emerging economies, particularly Iraq. The platform designed seamless QR-code checkouts, mobile merchant wallets, and transaction ledger synchronization.

THE ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEM

Extreme reliance on physical cash creates severe friction for Iraqi merchants, increases theft risks, and deprives retail businesses of formal credit history.

SOLUTION CONCEPT

A frictionless merchant application linked with a consumer digital wallet, enabling instant QR-based settlements and digital bookkeeping.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES
  • Instant point-of-sale QR code generation and dynamic payment scanning
  • Dual merchant/consumer mobile interfaces with offline receipt generation
  • Automated ledger reconciliation and end-of-day settlement reports
  • SMS and push-based transaction notifications with cryptographic verification
ARCHITECTURE & STACK

Mobile-first client apps connecting to secure RESTful payment microservices, transaction state machines, and relational ledger storage.
