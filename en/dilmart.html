DilMart

Iraq-Focused Digital Commerce Platform

OVERVIEW

DilMart is a digital commerce platform designed for the Iraqi market, combining consumer-facing shopping capabilities with an operational technology layer intended to support scalable digital retail. The platform addresses online shopping, order management, and the workflows associated with retail fulfillment in the Iraqi e-commerce context.

BUSINESS VALUE

A digital commerce platform combining a consumer shopping interface with an order management and fulfillment operations layer, designed specifically for the Iraqi market context.

SELECTED CAPABILITIES
  • Consumer-facing online shopping interface
  • Order management and retail fulfillment operations
  • Digital retail operations platform
