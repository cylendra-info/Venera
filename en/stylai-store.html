StylAI Store

Barber & Salon Management Platform — Provider App

OVERVIEW

StylAI Store (published on Google Play as StylAi Barber | إدارة الحلاقين) is the provider-facing component of the StylAI two-sided booking ecosystem. It enables barbershop and salon owners to manage incoming appointment requests, view and organize their daily schedule, and operate their booking pipeline through a dedicated mobile interface.

BUSINESS VALUE

A merchant-facing mobile management application paired with the StylAI consumer app, giving barbers and salon owners direct control over their appointment pipeline and provider profile.

SELECTED CAPABILITIES
  • Appointment request management for barbershop and salon owners
  • Schedule organization and booking pipeline management
  • Provider-side notifications for new and updated bookings
  • Integration with StylAI consumer booking flow as the two-sided marketplace counterpart
