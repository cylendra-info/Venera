StylAI

Barber & Salon Booking Platform — Consumer App

Customer app — discovery & booking
1 / 2
SYSTEM OVERVIEW

StylAI is the consumer-facing application of a two-sided barber and salon booking platform developed and published by Venera Finance LTD. Users discover nearby barbershops and salons through an interactive map, browse available services with pricing and durations, select a preferred barber, and confirm bookings — all from a single mobile interface.

THE ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEM

Booking a barber in emerging markets still relies on phone calls and walk-in visits, creating scheduling friction for both clients and service providers.

SOLUTION CONCEPT

A map-based mobile marketplace connecting customers with nearby barbershops and salons, enabling instant booking, tracking, and post-service ratings.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES
  • Map-based salon discovery with real salon photos on map pins
  • Service browsing with prices, durations, and barber selection
  • Instant appointment booking with status tracking (upcoming, completed, cancelled)
  • Push notifications for booking confirmations and status updates
  • Post-service experience ratings
  • Offers, rewards, and cashback availability
ARCHITECTURE & STACK

iOS and Android mobile applications. Active development evidenced by regular version releases (v1.1.3 with multiple incremental updates).
