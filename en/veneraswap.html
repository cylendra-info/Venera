VeneraSwap

Decentralized Automated Market-Maker Protocol

OVERVIEW

VeneraSwap represents an extensive blockchain engineering milestone associated with the Venera brand. Built around automated market-maker (AMM) algorithms, it enabled non-custodial token swaps, liquidity pool staking, and decentralized price discovery.

BUSINESS VALUE

A deterministic smart contract protocol governed by transparent mathematical formulas for automated price equilibrium, with zero centralized intermediary control.

SELECTED CAPABILITIES
  • Constant-product automated market maker (AMM) pricing logic
  • Non-custodial asset exchange with user wallet custody
  • Yield distribution algorithms for decentralized liquidity providers
  • Smart contract architecture designed to guard against reentrancy and arithmetic-overflow risks
