ROIC

Telegram-Based Digital Asset Utility Interface

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

ROIC explored conversational interfaces for decentralized asset interactions, allowing users to track prices, trigger alerts, and manage micro-transactions directly through secure Telegram bot commands.

THE ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEM

Navigating complex web3 interfaces poses a significant barrier for mainstream users in emerging communities who rely almost exclusively on messaging apps.

SOLUTION CONCEPT

Leveraging messaging bot infrastructure to provide an intuitive, text-and-menu driven gateway to digital asset information and utilities.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES
  • Automated real-time price feeds and volatility threshold alerts
  • Conversational command parser with inline interactive keyboards
  • Asynchronous webhook notification pipelines handling high concurrency
  • Community engagement mechanisms and automated participant verification
ARCHITECTURE & STACK

High-concurrency webhook ingestion workers built in Node.js/Python, integrated with external blockchain nodes and cache layers.
